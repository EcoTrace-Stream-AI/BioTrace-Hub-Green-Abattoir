##  EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

**BioTrace Hubs** is an open-architecture, decentralized AgTech bio-refinery system engineered to intercept and convert hazardous municipal abattoir waste into high-value, certified agricultural inputs. 

Deployed via flood-resilient, stilted 40ft High-Cube containerized units, the pilot node at the **Kumba Central Abattoir** (Meme Department, South West Region, Cameroon) addresses severe urban water pollution, public health risks, and methane emissions ($\text{CH}_4$) from unmanaged bovine slaughter waste.

## 🛠️ MODULAR CONTAINER & UTILITY ENGINEERING

The processing plant is fully self-contained and engineered for high resilience in equatorial wetland conditions:

* **Container Chassis:** Modified 40ft High-Cube shipping container ($12.192\text{m} \times 2.438\text{m} \times 2.896\text{m}$).
* **Stilted Substructure:** Elevated on a $1.5\text{m}$ stilted heavy-duty steel frame, providing flood immunity during seasonal tropical downpours.
* **Biosecurity Isolation:** Hermetically sealed internal partitions separating the **Red Zone** (Raw Material Intake) from the **Green Zone** (Processing & Packaging).
* **Off-Grid Power System:**
  * $5 \text{ kWp}$ Roof-Mounted Solar PV Array.
  * $10 \text{ kWh}$ LiFePO4 Energy Storage Bank.
  * $10 \text{ kVA}$ Automated Diesel Standby Generator Backup.
* **Environmental Safeguards:** $110\%$ capacity secondary bunded containment sumps prevent ground runoff in case of primary tank failure.

### Phase 2 Academic & Research Collaboration
Execution of a **3-to-6-month Cost-Capped Direct-Cost Shared Research Partnership** with the **Institute of Agricultural Research for Development (IRAD)** and the **University of Buea**:
1. **Soil & Agronomic Trials:** Testing crop response (cocoyam tubers, cassava, maize) under **Terra-Gold** and **Terra-Nutri** application.
2. **Animal Feed Bio-Efficacy:** Testing **Pro-Feed 10% Concentrate** in broiler and monogastric diets for Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) and live weight gain.
3. **Statutory Certification:** Compiling joint scientific evaluation dossiers for formal approval by **MINADER**, **MINEPIA**, and **ANOR** to enable sub-regional CEMAC trade and export under the **WillsMkt Global** brand.

## ⚖️ LEGAL FRAMEWORK & MUNICIPAL GOVERNANCE

* **Public-Private Partnership (PPP):** Authenticated under Law No. 2019/024 (General Code of Regional and Local Authorities) between **WillsMkt Organization** and the **Kumba City Council**, supported by the **Senior Divisional Officer (SDO Meme)**.
* **Institutional Continuity Clause:** All land allocations, waste diversion rights, and operational permits attach to the institutional **OFFICES** rather than individual officeholders, binding all future mayors and delegates.
* **Judicial Authentication:** Notarized (*Acte Authentique*) and formally registered at the **High Court Registry of Kumba** (*Tribunal de Grande Instance*).
* **OHADA Compliance:** Governed by OHADA Uniform Acts for commercial contract enforcement and investor indemnification protection.
